Designing for Trustworthiness

An action design research study on AI knowledge retrieval system redesign in a cultural institution — Lauri Lumme, Ulysseus Joint Master's Degree in AI for Business Transformation.

Evaluation results

Staff evaluation took place on 21 October 2026. Results are being written up and will be posted here.

What the study is about

A citation is generated text. It shows what the model claims, not what the sources say. This study builds a Finnish-language retrieval system over one museum's publications, adds a deterministic verifier that checks the answer against the passages actually retrieved, and evaluates with the institution's own staff whether such a check helps people tell a grounded answer from a fabricated one.

Commissioned by the Gösta Serlachius Fine Arts Foundation, Mänttä, Finland.
Supervisors: Dr Jorge Ropero, Dr María García Antuña (Universidad de Sevilla), Dr Lili Aunimo (Haaga-Helia).
